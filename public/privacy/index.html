What we do not collect

Disha has no account and no sign-up. The Android app and the website do not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or other tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Compass and motion

Compass and motion-sensor readings are processed on the device to show a direction. Disha does not send those readings to Ikshana Solutions or to any other server.

Location

If you allow it, Disha asks once for an approximate location. The app uses that reading on the device only, to calculate magnetic declination so the compass can point to true north. The coordinates and the declination are cached on the device. Disha does not transmit your location to our servers and does not share it with third parties.

Checks, scores, and saved homes

Quiz answers, headings, scores, and saved homes stay on the device (local storage on the web, app storage on Android and iOS). Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s data or the site data in the browser, deletes them. We do not receive a copy.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android, by the Apple App Store on iOS, and by Razorpay on the web. Ikshana Solutions receives only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Premium. We never receive your card number or other full payment credentials. On the web, restore uses the email you enter at checkout, and that email is used only to restore purchases.

Children

Disha is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change, and the new text will be posted at this same address. Continued use of Disha after the new date means you accept the update.
