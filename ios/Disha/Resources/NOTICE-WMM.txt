World Magnetic Model 2025 coefficients (wmm-2025.json)

Source: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information and the
National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, World Magnetic Model 2025.
The coefficient values are in the public domain.
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model

Disha uses them only as a fallback when Core Location cannot supply a
true heading. They are not a copy of the geomagnetism JavaScript package.
